## Reddit (r/n8n, r/automation, r/Python; check each subreddit's self-promo rules first)
Title: How I stopped forgetting to chase overdue invoices (free script, no SaaS)
Body: Late payments were my biggest cash-flow headache, and paid reminder apps felt like overkill. I wrote a small Python planner: export unpaid invoices to CSV, run one command, get a list of who to remind today, which tier (friendly/firm/final), and the message text, plus an aging report. Output is JSON so you can pipe it into n8n or any sender. It skips paid/disputed invoices and escalates big or very late ones to a personal call. Happy to take feedback on the escalation rules. Repo: https://github.com/Waqas-Ai-Engineer/invoice-payment-reminder-automation
