# Compliance notes (not legal advice)

- Keep reminders factual, polite and truthful; never threaten action you will not take.
- Rules on contacting debtors differ by country/state (for example consumer debt-collection laws). Business-to-business reminders are usually lighter-touch, but check local rules.
- Respect opt-outs and do not message disputed invoices (this tool skips them by default).
- Avoid sending outside reasonable hours; this tool does not send, so schedule your sender accordingly.
- Store customer data securely and do not commit it to Git.
